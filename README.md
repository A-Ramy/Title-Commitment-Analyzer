# Title Commitment Analyzer — Total Cash Home Buyers

An AI title agent: upload a title commitment and get every item that must be cleared before the deal can close,
grouped by priority, traced to the page it came from, and exportable to Google Sheets, Google Docs, CSV, Excel,
PDF and Word. The team trains it by uploading worked commitments with their own lists, and by reporting any
missing item from a live deal.

Status: **planning**. Read the plan in [`docs/PLAN.md`](docs/PLAN.md) and open the clickable draft screen
[`docs/draft/index.html`](docs/draft/index.html) in a browser (sample data only).

To rebuild the draft after editing `docs/draft/index.template.html`: `python3 docs/draft/build.py`.
